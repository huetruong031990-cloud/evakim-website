What is bún đậu mắm tôm? Ingredients and questions for your first order

White noodles and tofu arrive separately, beside a strongly scented sauce. Before mixing everything on your first bún đậu mắm tôm tray, look at the ingredients in its name.

Updated 2026-11-18

At a glanceBún means rice vermicelli, đậu here refers to tofu, and mắm tôm is fermented shrimp paste. Side dishes and portions vary, so do not assume every item pictured on a menu is included.

Separate the basics from optional extras

Rice noodles, fried tofu and shrimp paste form the core of the dish, with greens and accompaniments. Some combinations include meat or other extras. Ask what is included, what costs extra and how many people the order is intended to serve.

Ask for sauce on the side

Fermented shrimp paste has a distinctive aroma that may be unfamiliar. Ask whether the sauce can be served separately, then add it to suit your taste. You can ask about another sauce, but alternatives depend on the restaurant.

Tofu does not make the whole dish vegetarian

The dipping paste contains shrimp, and side dishes may contain meat. If you avoid an ingredient, ask about the sauce as well as every accompaniment. The actual ingredients matter more than the dish’s name.

Record three things before comparing prices

Note the restaurant and visit date, the combination ordered and the receipt total. A shared name does not make portions or extras identical. Separating ingredients, as in EvaKim’s food breakdown concept, creates more useful questions. This is an ordering guide, not a tasting review or a set of measured weights.
